Embedded Skill Modules & Algorithmic Specifications

Skill 1: PRISMA-S & Ambient Matrix Harmonizer cleanAmbientCorpus()

Parses multi-source CSV/TSV tables, flags experimental leaching/batch-adsorption rows (e.g., filtering 244 raw records to 134 ambient environmental records), standardizes coordinates and distance buffers (1, 5, 10 km), and handles non-detects via configurable censored imputation.

Skill 2: Automated 5-Rule Target Leakage Auditor runFiveRuleLeakageAudit()

Audits epidemiological survey + clinical registry datasets against: (R1) Deterministic spatial stratum constants, (R2) Post-outcome clinical treatment/follow-up items (e.g., item 6.11), (R3) Outcome-correlated missingness indicators (Fisher exact / Chi-square p < 0.001), (R4) Duplicate composite indices, and (R5) Temporal ascertainment shift (93 pre-linkage vs. 167 registry-linked cases).

Skill 3: Two-Dimensional Monte Carlo & Tail Diagnostics Engine run2DMonteCarloEngine()

Uses Float64 typed arrays and a deterministic Mulberry32 PRNG (seed 42) to run nested outer uncertainty loops (bootstrapping sample mean/GSD) and inner variability loops (individual IR, BW, EF, ED, SA). Computes Sobol first-order variance indices, Spearman rank correlations, and flags GSD > 5.0 small-sample lognormal tail inflation.

Skill 4: LOZO Spatial ML & Prior-Odds Brier Recalibrator runLozoSpatialAudit()

Trains L2-penalized logistic and gradient-boosted decision stumps across Leave-One-Zone-Out (LOZO) folds, computes within-zone pair-weighted concordance AUC, and applies Bayes prior-odds intercept recalibration to correct out-of-zone prevalence shift.
